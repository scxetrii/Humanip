A few things to know

AI Humanizer FAQ

Clear answers for a more thoughtful editing process.

What does an AI humanizer do?

An AI humanizer rewrites text to improve its phrasing, sentence structure and readability. It can help revise an AI-assisted draft, but the result still needs your review.

How is it different from a paraphrasing tool?

The two overlap. Paraphrasing tools restate text in different words, while AI humanizers typically emphasize natural phrasing, sentence variety and a less mechanical tone.

Can I use it for essays?

You can use it to help edit an essay draft where your course permits AI-assisted editing. Keep the ideas and analysis your own, verify citations and follow any disclosure requirements.

Will the revision keep my original meaning?

The goal is to retain your intended message while improving expression. Rewriting can still change nuances, so compare both versions and check important claims, terms and details.

Does humanizing text make it original?

Rewriting alone does not establish originality or replace attribution. Cite borrowed ideas and make sure your work includes your own reasoning and contribution.

Does the score match Turnitin or other AI detectors?

No. The naturalness meter and AI-pattern score are internal style estimates. Third-party detectors use their own models and can return very different results, so use these scores to guide editing rather than as a detector prediction.
